# @thebranding.ai — Style Study (from source material)

Derived from: 7-slide carousel "Viral Storytelling isn't talent" (444 comments,
1,213 shares) + 4 reels (32s–64s) supplied directly from the page. Reference
files in `posts/` and `frames/`.

## The system in one line
**Black screen. Ice-white condensed caps. One idea per beat. Real viral footage
as evidence. A coined term as the takeaway. A comment-keyword as the CTA.**

## Reel architecture (the signature format)
Two alternating layers, cut fast, driven by a voiceover whose words appear
on screen in sync:

1. **CASE FOOTAGE** — a real viral clip (BMW drift aerial, coffee-pour A/B,
   street stunt, AI-made sneaker spot) full-bleed 9:16. Often shown *as a
   screenshot of the platform UI* — like counts, usernames, comment numbers
   visible — because the UI chrome itself is the proof. Small word-sync
   captions (lowercase, white) land on the footage; hand-drawn white arrows
   point at details.
2. **BLACK TEACHING FRAMES** — pure #000 screen; 1–4 words at a time in bold
   condensed caps, ice white, centered; rapid word-by-word kinetic pacing
   ("BMW" → "GET" → "SO WHY DID IT WORK?" → "PATTERN INTERRUPT").

Recurring devices:
- **Framed phone inserts**: rounded-corner mini-phone screenshots floating on
  black, labeled above in caps ("NOTICE", "VERBAL", "UGC"); 2–3-up
  side-by-side comparisons with labels.
- **Big number payoff**: "8.5 MILLION", "12.8 MILLION", "30" set huge.
- **Coined term as thesis**: PATTERN INTERRUPT · HOOK SITUATION · 2 OPEN LOOPS
  — always caps, always the shareable takeaway.
- **One meme beat** max (e.g., Mr. Bean insert) for a laugh mid-lesson.
- Hooks stated as claims over footage: "THIS VIDEO PULLED 30 MILLION VIEWS."
- Duration 30–65s. Trending audio under VO.

## Carousel architecture
- Pure black bg; ice-white type; posted as video-carousel with music.
- **Slide 1**: giant 2-line condensed-caps claim ("VIRAL STORYTELLING ISN'T
  TALENT") + ONE physical macro-photo metaphor (a frayed thread).
- **Slide 2**: the metaphor becomes the diagram — thread splits into labeled
  strands (HOOK / OBSTACLE / LOOP / DELIVER). Framework = acronym.
- **Slides 3–6**: "H = HOOK" style headers (huge condensed caps); body in a
  clean neutral sans, white, generous leading; ❌ bad example vs ✅ good
  example in italics; bold for pivotal phrases; each slide ends with
  "TERM = plain-English definition."
- **Slide 7 (CTA)**: "COMMENT [KEYWORD]" + oversized keyword + lead-magnet
  promise ("5,000 psychology-based hooks"). Comment-gated DM delivery.
- Caption: one declarative sentence, no hashtag wall.

## Type
- **Display**: tall condensed grotesque caps (Anton / Oswald / Staatliches
  family). Tight leading. Occasionally soft drop shadow over footage.
- **Body**: neutral grotesque (Inter/Helvetica class), white, italics for
  quoted examples, bold for emphasis words.
- On-footage word-sync captions: small, mixed case, unobtrusive.

## Color
- #000000 ground. Ice white type (cool tint, ~#DCE7F2). No accent palette —
  the footage supplies all color. ❌/✅ emoji are the only semantic color.

## Voice
Analyst-teacher. Second person. Short declaratives. Coins terminology.
Quantified payoffs. Never hypey adjectives — the numbers do the hype.
Every piece ends in a comment-keyword lead magnet or a follow loop.

## PMO Media adaptation (real estate marketing)
Same skeleton, new evidence base: viral listing videos, agent brand reels,
brokerage ads, open-house campaigns as the case footage; coined terms for
real estate marketing psychology; comment-keyword magnets ("COMMENT LISTING
and I'll send the 50-hook listing pack"). The meta-pillar reels ("I built a
$10K listing shoot from my desk for $0") map 1:1 to their AI-workflow reel.
