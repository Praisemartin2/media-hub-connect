# PMO Media — Content Strategy v1

**Company:** PMO Media · real estate marketing
**Style north star:** @thebranding.ai (visual system finalizes on receipt of reference screenshots + PMO brand assets)
**Positioning:** *The marketing brain for real estate.* PMO Media shows agents, brokerages and investors how to market property like the world's best brands market products — with receipts (a 50-deliverable client engagement as the proof of work).

## Voice
Authoritative, compressed, visual-first. Short declarative lines. No filler, no "hey guys." Every post teaches one idea and lands one CTA. Big-brand fluency (Nike, Apple, Chanel) applied to open houses, listings, and agent brands.

## Content pillars

1. **Teardowns** — a listing ad, agent bio, brokerage brand or open-house campaign, dissected: what works, what kills it, the fix. (The signature @thebranding.ai format, real-estate-ified.)
2. **Big-brand lessons for realtors** — what a local agent can steal from Apple's product pages, Chanel's scarcity, Nike's storytelling, McDonald's consistency.
3. **Anatomy of a hook** — frame-by-frame breakdowns of real estate reels that stopped the scroll: first line, first frame, caption structure, CTA. (Cites PMO's own results once client stats sync.)
4. **Before / after makeovers** — a listing photo, caption, flyer or ad rebuilt in front of the viewer.
5. **The PMO process** — the meta story: how an AI-augmented one-person studio ships 50 pieces in 8 weeks. Builds authority + sells the service.
