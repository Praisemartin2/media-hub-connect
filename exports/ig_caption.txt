🚨 China's housing market just erased 20 YEARS of price growth.

Let that sink in. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in China have fallen back BELOW where they were in 2006 — down roughly 25% from the 2021 peak. 📉

Here's how it happened 👇

🔴 In 2020, Beijing dropped the "Three Red Lines" policy to force developers to cut their debt.
🔴 Evergrande — the biggest developer in the country, carrying $300B+ in debt — collapsed.
🔴 Tens of millions of units were left empty or unfinished. Entire ghost cities.
🔴 In early 2026, only 4 of 70 major cities saw new-home prices rise. In the resale market? Not a single one.

And here's the part nobody talks about: Chinese households had nearly 70% of their wealth tied up in housing. When the market fell, family wealth fell with it. Beijing even scrapped the policy this January — and prices are STILL falling.

So what does this mean for us investing here in the U.S.? 🇺🇸

💎 Lesson 1: Real estate does NOT only go up. If your deal only works with appreciation, you don't have a deal — you have a bet.

💎 Lesson 2: Cash flow is the foundation. Appreciation is the bonus. Buy properties that pay you every month, and a downturn can't force your hand.

💎 Lesson 3: Watch supply. China overbuilt by tens of millions of homes. The U.S. is the opposite story — we're still millions of homes SHORT. Same asset, completely different fundamentals.

💎 Lesson 4: Diversify how you hold wealth. 70% in one asset class is how a correction becomes a catastrophe.

Real estate is still the most powerful wealth-building tool available — when you buy it right, for cash flow, in markets with real demand. 🔑
