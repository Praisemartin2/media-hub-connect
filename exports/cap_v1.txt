I lost $60,000 lending money to a house flipper. 💸

And it wasn't because the flip went bad. It was because of ONE document I didn't have.

By 2021 I'd already done three deals with this flipper — even brought my family into two of them. We were making 20% returns in 3–4 months. Everything looked great.

Then in 2022 she went after a bigger flip. Comps looked good, numbers were solid… until interest rates nearly DOUBLED (3.5% → 7%). That luxury property sat on the market for over a year and the profit got squeezed to nothing.

Here's the real mistake 👇

We wired the money directly to her with just a signed promissory note — instead of sending it through the title company and recording a LIEN as a second mortgage on the property.

So when she finally sold, she kept the proceeds, paid back none of her private lenders, and left the state. Now we're paying lawyers to chase it.

Today I do it completely differently. I just lent private money to another investor — everything went through the attorneys, signed and notarized, with the lien recorded on the property. If they don't perform, we foreclose. When they sell, we get paid first. No handshake deals. No compromise. 🔒

That's the difference between an agent who's read about investing and one who's actually lost money doing it — and learned.
