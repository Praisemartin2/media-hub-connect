## EP4 — "New Management" (~48s of shots)

1. **(8s) The news.** Marcus leans on Derek's desk, office buzzing behind them. Marcus, grinning: "Breaking news. The boss just got fired. Escorted out with his little box." Derek, hopeful: "New boss starts today. Everyone says she's amazing."
2. **(8s) The arrival.** Diane addresses the office, polished and warm: "I'm thrilled to be here. I believe in people... and efficiency. Speaking of which—" (she lifts a folder)
3. **(8s) The cut.** Diane, flat and pleasant, reading names: "Gene. Patricia. Dave from accounting. HR will see you now. Today is your last day." Stunned office, frozen faces.
4. **(9s) Gene's exit.** Gene at his desk holding a small cardboard box: "Thirty-eight years. Two strokes at this desk. And it all fits in one box." Quietly: "They kept the stapler."
5. **(9s) The lesson.** Confessional Marcus, serious, no smirk: "Dave just had a baby. Loyalty didn't protect him. A job can end in one morning — your bills don't. That's why I buy rentals. Rent doesn't fire you."
6. **(6s) The turn.** Derek picks the book up off Marcus's desk, determined: "Okay. Teach me. Before it's my name on that list." Marcus nods once.

## EP5 — "The First Door" (~44s of shots)

1. **(8s) The wake-up.** Derek confessional, empty desks visible behind him: "Three empty desks in one day. I did the math — my savings last four months. That woke me up better than coffee ever has."
2. **(8s) The call.** Derek hunched at his desk on lunch, whispering into his phone: "Pre-approved? For the duplex on Maple? ...Book the showing Saturday." He glances around, hangs up smiling.
3. **(8s) The math.** Marcus and Derek over a laptop. Marcus: "Unit A rents for fourteen hundred. The mortgage is nineteen. You live in Unit B for five hundred a month." Derek: "That's less than my parking spot."

6. **(5s) The step.** Derek at the window, keys-to-the-future energy: "Saturday. First showing. First door." Marcus: "First of many."
