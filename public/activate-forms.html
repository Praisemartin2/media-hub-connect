Activate form delivery

This one-time step connects the website's forms to cofyincorporated@gmail.com. Only COFY staff need to do this, and only once.

  1. Press the button below. FormSubmit's page will open and send an activation email.
  2. Open cofyincorporated@gmail.com (check Spam/Promotions too), find the email from FormSubmit, and click Activate.

After that, every contact message, volunteer signup and newsletter signup from the website arrives in the inbox automatically.

If the activation email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, look in Spam, then try this button once more.
