The official flyer is square and is the primary post image on Instagram and
Facebook. It is on the site as the event's artwork and at
`https://www.cofyouth.org/media/webinar-2026-11-27.jpg`.

These three are brand-matched companions, mainly for the places a square
flyer does not fit:

| Square alternative | `public/media/promo/square-1x1.png` | 1080 × 1080 |

and run:

