> A child who cannot yet read a word problem is not a child who cannot think.
>
> Those two things get confused constantly, in classrooms and at kitchen
> tables, and the usual response is to hand the young person easier work.
> Easier work is not the same as a better way in.
>
> Problem Based Learning starts from the opposite assumption: give a young
> person a real problem worth solving, and build the thinking around it. For
> youth with developmental delays, that shift is often the difference between
> compliance and genuine understanding.
